# GAME SPEC — GAMIFIED VERSION

## Project

**English Prepositions Space Missions**  
Author: **Katerina Zhuchkova**  
Level: **A1**

## Main goal

Practise the prepositions:

- in
- on
- under
- behind
- next to
- in front of
- between
- near

## Story

The learner helps the cat complete three space missions and earn the title
**Preposition Master**.

## Mission 1 — Sentence Builder

Mission goal: repair the sentence console.

The learner:

- looks at the picture;
- arranges sentence parts;
- checks the answer;
- earns points, stars and a badge.

The task with `between the table and the box` is excluded from this mission
because there is no complete object audio for it.

## Mission 2 — Picture Shooter

Mission goal: restore the location map.

- Easy: 3 pictures.
- Medium: 4 pictures in a 2×2 grid.
- Hard: 6 pictures.
- The prompt shows a full phrase, for example `under the table`.

## Mission 3 — Type the Preposition

Mission goal: enter the secret words.

- Easy and Medium automatically play the target preposition.
- A replay-audio button is available.
- Hard does not provide the audio hint.

## Gamification system

- missions unlock sequentially;
- stars are saved in Local Storage;
- each mission gives 0–3 stars;
- three achievement badges;
- combo for correct answers in a row;
- bonus points for combo;
- mission progress 0/3;
- total stars 0/9;
- final Preposition Master screen;
- teacher mode can unlock all missions;
- reset-progress button.

## Existing mechanics preserved

- Easy / Medium / Hard;
- Slow / Normal / Fast;
- score;
- lives;
- timer;
- music and sounds controls;
- rules;
- pause and restart;
- responsive design for phone, tablet and desktop.
